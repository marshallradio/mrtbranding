Marshall Radio Telemetry · Brand hub

Welcome to the Marshall Radio brand hub

If you're here, you shape how Marshall shows up in the world: on a product page, on a trade-show table at a field meet, on a receiver box or in a two-line email to a falconer who just lost sight of their bird.

Marshall Radio Telemetry designs and supplies premium GPS long-range tracking and recovery equipment to hunters, parrot free fliers, rocketry, and competitors around the world. This hub keeps every line, from falconry and parrots to sporting dogs and apparel, recognizably one company: the logo, the colors, the type, the voice and the naming rules.

Dip in when you need a file, a hex code or a quick sense-check.
