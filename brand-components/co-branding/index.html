Co-branding & Logo Lockups

Rules for appearing alongside partners, retailers, and sponsors.

Horizontal lockup

Marshall Radio logo left, partner right, thin vertical rule between them. Both logos optically equal height. Clear space of 1× the height of the “M” around the entire lockup.

Stacked lockup

Use only when horizontal space is limited. Marshall Radio above the partner, centered.

Rules

  • Marshall Radio leads unless the partner is the primary host.
  • Keep Marshall Radio monochrome when the partner has strong color.
  • Never create combined marks or place logos inside each other.
  • Sponsor walls: equal optical size, consistent baseline.
