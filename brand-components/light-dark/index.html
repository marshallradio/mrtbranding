Light and Dark Modes

Light is the default. Dark is for high-focus or photographic contexts. Every component needs both treatments.

Light

White or Warm White ground. Ink text. Primary Blue actions.

White
Warm White
Ink text
Primary Blue

Dark

Ink or Ink Deep ground. White text. Primary Blue actions.

Ink
Ink Deep
White text
Primary Blue
  • Never use pure black (#000) or pure white in large fields unless photographic.
  • Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Large text and UI graphics ≥ 3:1.
  • Focus rings remain visible in both modes.
