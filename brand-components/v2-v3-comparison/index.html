V2 / V3 comparison

Current V2 direction versus a proposed V3 with refined colors and a cleaner arcs treatment. The arcs remain the single graphic identity; everything else is tightened.

V2 · Current

Inherited palette

Primary
Secondary

Heavy navy and dusty red. Arcs appear as soft grey photo overlays in footers. Feels closer to the previous decade’s site.

V3 · Proposed

Refined, lighter system

Primary
Secondary

Deeper, cleaner blue and a more precise accent. Arcs as a thin vector stroke at very low opacity. Quieter luxury, higher contrast.

Arcs treatment

V2

Soft overlay

Large grey or white arcs as photographic or heavily transparent backgrounds. Often taken directly from older site assets. Can feel soft and dated at small sizes.

V3

Precise gesture

Single thin vector arc, large and cropped. Very low opacity (4–8%). Blue or near-black on light; white on dark. Never filled. Same identity, sharper execution.

Geometric pattern notes

  • Quiet luxury — tone-on-tone arcs work better than high-contrast patterns for this brand.
  • Fluid geometry — keep the arc as a single sweeping curve; avoid repeating it as a pattern.
  • Micro-scale — a faint arcs device can live in large backgrounds or section dividers without competing with photography.
  • Avoid checkerboards, dense geometric tiles, or maximalist repeats — they fight the single-arc rule.

This page is a working comparison only. Nothing here replaces the current guidelines until the direction is approved.
