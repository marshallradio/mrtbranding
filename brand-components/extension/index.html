Key extensions added

Light and dark modes

Light is default (white / Warm White ground, Ink text). Dark for high-contrast panels and photographic heroes. Every component needs both treatments. No pure black or white in large fields.

Accessibility

44×44 px touch targets, visible focus rings, ≥ 4.5:1 body contrast, reading width ≤ 68 characters, RTL via logical properties.

Layout and spacing

4 px spacing scale. Section padding 64–96 px. One primary action. Flat cards at 20 px radius. One hero per page.

Motion

Instant 80–120 ms, hover 200 ms, menus 220–300 ms, panels 360–500 ms, reveals 1000–1300 ms. No bounce. Hover desktop-only.

Iconography & product visuals

24 px icon grid. Clean line drawings in Primary Blue or Ink for diagrams. Arcs only as faint background (4–8 % opacity).

Data visualization

Primary Blue for the main series. Signal Red only for alerts. Always show units. Maximum four series. Works in light and dark.

Templates (next)

Presentation decks, social kit, email signatures, letterhead, product one-pagers.
