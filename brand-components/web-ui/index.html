Web application UI

Guidelines for tables, paragraphs, buttons, forms, diagrams, and system messages. Use Matter Regular (400) and Bold (700), the type scale, and brand colors.

Paragraphs

Body text uses Matter Regular at 16px with 1.6 line-height. Keep line length under ~75 characters for readability. Use sentence case.

Second paragraph. Avoid justified text. Generous spacing between paragraphs (24px).

Buttons

Primary: M Blue fill, white Bold label. Secondary: outline. Destructive: M Red. Minimum height 44px for touch. Use sentence case.

Forms

Enter a valid email address.

Labels Bold 700. Inputs 16px. Error text M Red, 13px, below the field. Focus ring M Blue.

Form validation messages

Required field.
Must be at least 8 characters.
Saved successfully.

Keep messages short, specific, and in sentence case. Place directly under the field. Use M Red for errors.

Product diagrams and illustrations

Use clean line drawings or simple isometric views. Prefer real product photography when available. Diagrams should use M Blue for primary elements, Black for outlines, and Light Grey for backgrounds. Avoid gradients, drop shadows, and decorative effects. Keep labels in Matter Bold or Regular at 13–16px.

Illustrations of the arcs device may appear large and faint as a background element, never as a competing logo.

Summary rules

  • Matter Regular 400 for body and inputs; Bold 700 for labels, buttons, and headings.
  • Follow the type scale. Optional tighter tracking on large headings only.
  • M Blue for primary actions and headers; M Red only for destructive or error states.
  • On black backgrounds use Light Grey text.
  • Keep messages short, specific, and in sentence case.
  • High contrast, generous spacing, no decorative effects.
