Web application UI

Lighter, outline-driven interface patterns. Use Matter Regular (400) and Bold (700), the type scale, and restrained brand color.

Paragraphs

Body text uses Matter Regular at 16px with open line-height. Keep line length under ~75 characters. Use sentence case.

Second paragraph. Avoid justified text. Generous vertical rhythm.

Buttons

Prefer outline buttons. Primary uses M Blue border and text. Destructive uses M Red outline only. Avoid heavy fills.

Product diagrams and illustrations

Prefer clean line drawings or simple isometric views. Use M Blue for primary strokes, Black for secondary outlines, and very light grey backgrounds. Avoid gradients, heavy fills, and drop shadows. Labels in Matter Regular or Bold at 13–16px.

The arcs device may appear large and faint as a background element only.

Summary rules

  • Matter Regular 400 for body and inputs; Bold 700 for labels and emphasis.
  • Prefer outlines over fills for buttons, bars, and containers.
  • Light borders (Black 20%) and soft focus rings.
  • M Blue and M Red used sparingly for emphasis and errors only.
  • Generous spacing, high contrast, no decorative effects.
