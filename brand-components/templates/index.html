Templates

Consistent starting points for presentations, social, email, and documents.

  • Presentations — Helvetica LT Pro titles, Source Sans body, light and dark versions
  • Social media — 1:1 and 9:16 with photography scrims and white type
  • Email signatures — clean, small logo, Source Sans contact details
  • Stationery — letterhead, business cards, proposals
  • Events — badges and stage graphics following co-branding rules
