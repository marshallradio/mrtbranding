Data Visualization & Product Diagrams

Clear, accurate charts and diagrams for telemetry products.

  • Primary series: Primary Blue. Alerts: Signal Red only.
  • Maximum four series. Always show units.
  • Charts must work in light and dark.
  • Product diagrams: clean line drawings, Primary Blue or Ink strokes.
  • Arcs only as faint background (4–8% opacity).
