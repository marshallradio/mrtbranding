# Data Visualization & Product Diagrams

Telemetry products generate range, frequency, battery, and location data. Visualizations must be clear, accurate, and on-brand.

## Chart rules

- Primary series: Primary Blue (#487FFF).
- Secondary series: Ink or a muted blue.
- Alert / out-of-range: Signal Red only.
- Maximum four series per chart.
- Always show units (miles, MHz, %, etc.). Never convert units silently.
- Axis labels and legends in Source Sans Pro, 12–14 px.
- Charts must work in both light and dark modes.
- Provide a text summary or data table for accessibility.

## Product diagrams

- Clean line drawings or simple isometric views.
- Primary strokes in Primary Blue or Ink.
- Light grey or Warm White backgrounds.
- No gradients or heavy shadows.
- Labels in Source Sans Pro or Helvetica Bold at 13–16 px.
- The arcs may appear large and faint (4–8 % opacity) as a background only.

## UI product visuals

- Crop to the relevant interface.
- Use a simple rounded frame.
- Place on a light or photographic background with a controlled scrim.
- No OS chrome or 3D tilts unless required for realism.
