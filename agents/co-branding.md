# Co-branding & Logo Lockups

Marshall Radio Telemetry appears alongside partners, retailers, event sponsors, and technology partners. These rules keep the brand clear and consistent when it shares space with others.

## Logo lockups

### Primary lockup
Wordmark only. Use on light or dark backgrounds with sufficient contrast. Preferred for most applications.

### Horizontal lockup (with partner)
Place the Marshall Radio logo on the left. Partner logo on the right. Separate with a thin vertical rule in Ink or white at 40% opacity, height equal to the wordmark.

- Minimum clear space around the entire lockup: 1× the height of the “M” in Marshall.
- Both logos optically equal in height.
- Never stack logos vertically unless space forces it (then use the stacked lockup).

### Stacked lockup
Marshall Radio logo above the partner logo, centered. Use only when horizontal space is limited (e.g., vertical banners, narrow social posts).

### Technology / capability lockups
When showing compatibility (e.g., with a radio brand or mapping software), use a simple “works with” or “compatible with” line in Source Sans Pro above or beside the partner logo. Do not modify either logo.

## Co-branding rules

- Marshall Radio logo always leads (left or top) unless the partner is the primary host of the event or publication.
- Do not place logos inside each other or create combined marks.
- Maintain clear space. Never let partner logos or text enter the clear space of the Marshall Radio logo.
- On dark backgrounds use the white logo. On light backgrounds use the Ink logo.
- Color partners: if the partner logo contains strong color, keep Marshall Radio monochrome (Ink or white) so it remains distinct.
- Sponsor walls: equal optical size, consistent baseline, generous spacing. Group by tier if required (title, gold, silver) but keep Marshall Radio treatment consistent within each tier.

## Don’t

- Stretch, recolor, or add effects to either logo.
- Place the Marshall Radio logo smaller than the partner when we are co-equal.
- Use the arcs or photography as a background for a co-branded lockup.
- Add drop shadows or outlines to make logos “pop.”

## Approval
Co-branded applications that will be used publicly (packaging, websites, large events) should be reviewed by the brand team before release.
