Rules that always apply: `/AGENTS.md`

## Company
| Context | Write |
|---|---|
| First mention, formal, legal, footer | Marshall Radio Telemetry |
| Following mentions | Marshall Radio, or Marshall |
| Regional site names (as published) | Marshall Radio Worldwide, Marshall Radio Europe, Marshall Radio United Kingdom, Marshall Radio Gulf, Marshall Radio China |
| Don't write | MRT (in customer copy), Marshall Telemetry, Marshall Radio Telemetry Inc. (no legal suffix is published) |

Note: "Marshall" alone is shared with unrelated brands (for example Marshall amplifiers). In a cold context, such as a headline, ad or social bio, use the full name first.

## Rules
- Copy product names exactly, including capitals: **PocketLink** (the warranty page also writes "Pocketlink"; use the catalog form). **Field Marshall** is two words.
- The site writes both "Turbo GPS" and "Turbo-GPS" (parrot product). Use "Turbo GPS" unless quoting the parrot product title.
- Frequencies are capitalized acronyms: VHF, UHF, GPS, GNSS, RF.
- Don't create new product or feature names, model numbers or versions. Ask Marshall Radio Telemetry first.
- Generic terms stay lowercase: transmitter, receiver, tracking collar, tail piece, backpack mount.
- URLs and file names: lowercase and hyphenated, matching the site's slugs: `/ww/product-category/gps-system/`, `turbo-gps-transmitter`.
