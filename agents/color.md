## Tints (20% step system)

## Rules
- Our brand is always represented in these colors. Do not use any other or unauthorized colors, and do not change or adjust them.
- Avoid true black and true white where possible; use Black `#171D1A` and Light Grey `#F1F1F1`.
- Tints are for effects, hover states and secondary illustration tones, never as primary colors.
- Approved pairings: Blue text with the color icon on Khaki Grey; white/Light Grey text and icon on Blue; Khaki Grey text on Blue; white text on Black; dark grey text on Khaki Grey; white text on Red (use red backgrounds sparingly).
- Don't pair Blue with Black or dark greys, Blue with Red, or set important text in Blue on Red (contrast too low).
- Keep a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for text (Khaki Grey and Light Grey are background colors on white, not text colors).

## Web usage on this hub
- Canvas White or Light Grey, headings M Blue, body Black, section headings M Red.
- Dark bands use Black or M Blue with Light Grey text; accents on dark use Red 40 `#D0ACA5` (8.25:1 on Black).
- Buttons and links M Blue, hover Blue 80. Captions Black 80 `#454A48`; secondary labels Black 60 `#747776` (4.52:1).
- Charts: M Blue first series, M Red highlight, Khaki Grey and Blue 40 for context.
