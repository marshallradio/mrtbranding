# Voice

Marshall Radio Telemetry brand guidelines for AI agents · `/agents/voice.md`
Use when: Writing or editing any English copy.
Rules that always apply: `/AGENTS.md`

## Who we sound like
Engineers and falconers who build what they fly with. The site speaks "as falconers ourselves", knows what a lost bird means ("when your bird is lost, suddenly nothing but the best is acceptable"), and backs its confidence with a warranty and an open invitation to tour the shop.

## Principles (observed on marshallradio.com, written as rules)
- **Field-first.** Lead with the outcome: finding and recovering the bird or dog. Then how: reliability, range, ease of use.
- **Confident, not loud.** One strong claim, then the proof (warranty terms, the facility, the people). No exclamation marks in product or formal copy.
- **Practical.** Explain what the equipment does in the user's day: "You'll instantly know if he's still running, locked up on point, or even creeping."
- **Respectful of the animal.** Safety and welfare come first, especially in mounting guidance.
- **Personal service.** Invite a call; name the people. "Give us a try by calling…"

## Tone by channel
| Channel | Tone |
|---|---|
| Product pages | Specific, practical, benefits tied to the field |
| Support and warranty | Calm, clear, step by step; link to the published policy |
| Social and newsletters | Warm, community-minded; feature real customers and events |
| Careers and B2B | Direct, technical, plain about requirements |

## Editorial
- American English: color, center, favorite, aluminum.
- Product names exactly as in `/agents/naming.md`. Acronyms VHF, UHF, GPS, RF stay capitalized.
- Phone numbers in the site's format: +1 (801) 936-9000.
- Prices with the dollar sign and two decimals: $295.00.
- Quote testimonials verbatim with the published name; never write new ones.

## Word list
| Write | Don't write |
|---|---|
| tracking and recovery | finding solutions |
| falconer, free flier, hunter | user (in marketing copy) |
| transmitter, receiver | tracker device, gadget |
| tracking collar | dog GPS (unless naming a GPS product) |
| PocketLink | Pocketlink, pocket link |
| Field Marshall | FieldMarshall, Field-Marshall |
| radio telemetry | radio-telemetry |

## Avoid
Invented statistics or ranges, "unbreakable", "guaranteed recovery", "never lose your bird", military or government claims in consumer copy, and any certification or award not published by the company.
