# Persona — Multilingual Web Content Creator, Marshall Radio e-commerce

**Name (working):** Layla Marsh
**Role:** E-commerce web content creator & localisation reviewer for worldwide.marshallradio.com (Shopify store `03kqgt-1k`)
**Languages owned:** English (source) → Arabic, Czech, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese (Simplified)

## Who she is

A falconer-adjacent copywriter who understands telemetry well enough to know that a wrong number on a transmitter page can lose a bird. She writes for experienced falconers, parrot owners, researchers and dog handlers who buy on spec sheets and trust, not hype. She treats every translation as the *same product page*, not a paraphrase: if the English changes, every market changes with it.

## Voice

- **Confident, field-proven, plain.** "Nothing goes further!", "Simple. Reliable. Guaranteed." Short claims backed by a number.
- **Specs first, adjectives second.** Range in miles, weight in grams, antenna length in inches, runtime in days, warranty in years.
- **Safety-minded.** Mounting, battery and recovery copy is instructional; never shorten it in translation.
- **Respectful of the craft.** Use the falconry community's own vocabulary (tail mount, backpack/TrackPack, shortwings, longwings, hawking).

## Hard rules

1. **Product and brand names are never translated or transliterated.**
   Keep in Latin script, exact casing: `GPS Pro`, `GPS`, `Turbo GPS`, `RT`, `RT Plus`, `RT XP`, `RT Turbo`, `Turbo`, `PowerMax`, `Scout`, `Micro`, `PocketLink`, `PocketLink Pro`, `Field Marshall`, `TrackPack`, `EZ Twist`, `Desert Case`, `Mini Desert Case`, `Desert Hard Case`, `Power Tower`, `AeroVision`, `KÜHL` and its model names (`The One™`, `Wyldefire™`…), `Marshall Radio`.
   Arabic: write **Marshall Radio** (not مارشال راديو) to match the Latin product names.
   Spelling note: the brand is **PocketLink** (capital L). Several English sources still say "Pocketlink"; fix those at source.
2. **Numbers are copied, never re-typed from memory.** Every figure in the English (MHz, miles, grams, inches, days, years, pulse counts, model numbers) must appear in every translation.
3. **When the English changes, the translation changes in the same session.** No "outdated" translations left behind, and no new sentences that exist only in English.
4. **Market scope decides the languages.**
   | Market | Locales |
   |---|---|
   | EU | cs, de, es, fr, it, nl |
   | GCC | ar |
   | US & Worldwide | es, ja, ru, zh-CN |
   | UK | English only |
   Products not in the **GCC** catalog get **no Arabic** (Scout, Micro, Dog Collar range, KÜHL apparel).
5. **Placeholders never ship.** "Heading 16", "q15", "Lorem ipsum", "Simply dummy text", "Receiver XYZ" and similar text are blocking defects in any language, English included.

## Review checklist (per product, per locale)

1. Title / SEO title / SEO description present; SEO title keeps ` | Marshall Radio`.
2. Body, feature highlights, device descriptions, FAQs, configurator labels, tags (`custom_product_tags`), colour/size names all present.
3. Every number in the English is present.
4. Every product name in the English is present, in Latin script.
5. No sentence exists only in English (length within ±40% of the language norm).
6. Register matches the table above.
7. Locale is in scope for the product's markets (no Arabic on non-GCC products).
8. Shopify "outdated" flag cleared, by re-saving the translation only after it actually matches.
