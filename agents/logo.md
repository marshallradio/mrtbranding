## Anatomy and lockup
- Icon: the M Red ring with three M Blue arcs. Wordmark: **Marshall** (based on a modified Helvetica Neue Medium Italic) in M Blue, **RADIO TELEMETRY** in M Red beneath.
- The icon ring is exactly the height of the wordmark; the gap between icon and wordmark equals the wordmark height; the right point of the icon aligns with the bottom of the wordmark.
- The logo is locked artwork: never retype, redraw or rearrange it.

## Clear space
- At a minimum, keep clear space equal to the height of the Marshall icon on all four sides. Each version uses its own icon height to measure clear space.

## Minimum size
- Primary lockup: 2 cm (0.75 in) high in print, 50 px high on screen.
- Icon: 2 cm in print, 50 px on screen. Wordmark alone: 2 cm in print, 18 px on screen.
- Never reproduce the logo smaller than these sizes.
- Header exception: the site header shows the logo at 30px high; everywhere else the 50px minimum applies.

## Color and backgrounds
- Two-color versions on solid-color backgrounds; pick dark or light for maximum contrast.
- Photographs: one-color light version on dark photos, one-color dark version on light photos. Never the two-color version on photographs.
- Low-contrast patterns: one-color version. On imagery, place the logo where it doesn't compete with the subject.

## Don'ts
- Don't stretch, squash, skew or distort the logo.
- Don't edit the logo colors, use off-brand colors or reduce opacity.
- Don't add graphic effects, including drop shadows.
- Don't place it on high-contrast patterns or busy photographs.
- Don't change the layout or relationship between logo elements.
- Don't encroach on the clear space.

## Placement
- Top left on web pages, documents and title slides; the footer repeats the logo with the address block.
