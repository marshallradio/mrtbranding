Imagery and the arcs

Marshall Radio Telemetry brand guidelines for AI agents · /agents/imagery.md Use when: Choosing or creating visuals. Rules that always apply: /AGENTS.md

Photography (what the site uses)

  • Raptors in flight or on the glove in open country (homepage hero: a Harris's hawk over desert hills; feature band: a gyrfalcon with a backpack-mounted transmitter on Black).
  • Parrots in free flight, pointing dogs in the field, falconers in Marshall apparel, people in conversation outdoors.
  • Natural light, real places, equipment visible where possible. Muted, cool skies.
  • Never: AI-generated birds, dogs or equipment; stock studio pets; injured or distressed animals; transmitters mounted incorrectly (see Mounting Methods on the site).

The arcs

  • The swept arcs of the logo are the brand's only graphic device.
  • Use very large, cropped off the edge, in white at 20–40% opacity over sky, or light grey (#F1F1F1–#F1F1F1) on white, as on the site hero and footer (footer-arcs.jpg).
  • One arcs device per layout. Don't animate, outline or recolor it Red when it is used as a background.

Icons

Use Font Awesome solid or regular icons in M Blue or Grey; no other icon set is published.

Illustration and icons

one abstract SVG per guideline section in /assets/artwork/ and a 2px rounded line-icon set in M Blue in /assets/icons/, hand-drawn for this hub from the logo's arcs and ring, radio waves, signal lines and GPS contours. Only the palette and 20% tints, following the approved pairings (no Blue with Red, no Blue with Black). Use them for navigation tiles and small UI; never in place of real photography or the logo. Also in the brand kit.
