The Worldwide site offers English, Japanese, Russian and Spanish language switches; the Videos page lists English, Español, Français, Deutsch and 中文. A Spanish and Portuguese contact exists for Mexico and South America (see the Contact page).

## Rules
- Keep "Marshall Radio Telemetry" and every product name in Latin script inside Arabic, Chinese or Japanese text.
- Non-English copy must be written or reviewed by a native speaker. Don't publish machine translation; some current regional copy reads as machine translated and should not be reused as a model.
- Arabic layouts are right to left: mirror the grid and directional icons, not the logo, numbers, product codes or phone numbers.
- Prices and policies differ by region; never copy US prices or shipping offers to other regions.
