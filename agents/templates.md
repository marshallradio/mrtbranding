# Templates

Ready-to-use templates keep presentations, social posts, email, and documents consistent without starting from scratch.

## Presentations

- Title slides: Helvetica LT Pro Bold, large, Ink or white on photography or solid Primary Blue.
- Content slides: Source Sans Pro body, clear hierarchy, generous margins.
- Dark mode versions available for stage use.
- One idea per slide. No more than six bullets.
- Charts follow the data visualization rules.

## Social media

- Post (1:1) and story (9:16) templates.
- Photography with controlled scrim and white type for heroes.
- Short headline in Helvetica LT Pro Bold, supporting line in Source Sans Pro.
- Logo bottom-left or bottom-right with clear space.
- Maximum two lines of headline.

## Email signatures

- Name in Helvetica LT Pro Bold or Source Sans Pro Medium.
- Title and contact in Source Sans Pro.
- Small logo or wordmark.
- No large graphics or multiple colors.

## Stationery & documents

- Letterhead: logo top-left, contact details in footer in Source Sans Pro.
- Business cards: front with logo and name, back with contact.
- Proposals and one-pagers: consistent header, Primary Blue accents only.

## Events

- Name badges, agendas, and stage graphics use the same type and color rules as the main brand.
- Sponsor walls follow co-branding rules.

## Generators
Future: simple tools to generate email signatures and social posts from approved templates.
