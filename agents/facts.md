## Company
- What we do: "Marshall Radio Telemetry designs and supplies premium GPS long-range tracking and recovery equipment to hunters, parrot free fliers, rocketry, and competitors around the world."
- Promise: "Choose Marshall falconry transmitters, dog tracking collars and direction finding receivers and you'll have the most carefully engineered and reliable tracking system available."
- Mission: "Our mission is to supply the best possible tracking and recovery systems to hunters and competitors around the world." Quality means three things: reliability, range, and ease of use.
- Heritage: write "since 1997" only in that sense; no other founding date is published.
- Careers copy: "Marshall Radio Telemetry is the world leader in miniature Radio and GPS tracking systems." and "Vertically integrated, Marshall Radio designs, engineers, manufactures, markets, and services our own products."
- Facility: customers can tour the facilities, including product assembly, the environmental testing chamber, the Faraday cage and the machine shop.
- Careers headline: "No One goes to the Lengths We Do".
- Company attitude: "as falconers ourselves".

## Warranty
- Receivers and PocketLinks: three years (mechanical and moving parts, and electronics) from date of purchase.
- Dog Collar Transmitters: three years from date of purchase, to the original owner.
- Transmitters: one year (Scout); three years (Turbo GPS, RT GPS, Micro, RT Plus, RT, PowerMax); five years (Turbo).
- The Our Mission page refers to "a three year warranty". When in doubt, link to the Warranties page rather than paraphrasing.
