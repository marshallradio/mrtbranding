Sub-brands

One Binghalib, many companies. Each company keeps its own mark and colours, and sits under the Binghalib emblem through the family name or an endorsement.

Family-name companies

Binghalib Engineering, Qatar, Trading and Technology use the bilingual Binghalib lockup in group Indigo and Red. The family name is the endorsement.

Endorsed companies

TAMCO, Rolli, VBG Intech, Intech, SYSCOM, BMS, Marshall, MEM, AlphaTech and Time Cargo keep their own marks and palettes, endorsed with "A member of Binghalib Group of Companies" proposed.
