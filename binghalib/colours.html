Colours

Indigo is our signature

Binghalib Indigo carries the logo, the catalogue's sidebar and title bars, and every dark surface. Binghalib Red appears in the emblem's keyline and the Arabic name. Use it as an accent, not as a background. The four domain colours come from the Group Profile's services matrix. They identify Manufacturing, Engineering, Trading and Services, so use them for wayfinding only.
