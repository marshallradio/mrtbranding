# Photography and icons

Binghalib brand guidelines for AI agents · https://sbinghalib.github.io/binghalib/agents/imagery.md · Updated 2026-10-19
Use when: Choosing photos or drawing icons.
Rules that always apply: https://sbinghalib.github.io/binghalib/AGENTS.md

## Photography
- Show real factories, real panels and real landmark projects. Shoot bright and clean: yellow epoxy floors, white cabinets, daylight architecture. Avoid generic stock.
- Available (low-res extracts, to be replaced): `/assets/img/factory-floor.jpg`, `lv-panel.jpg`, `project-dubai-eye.jpg`, `tamco-facade.jpg`, `tamco-interior.jpg`, `falcon-tracking.jpg`, `satellite.jpg`.
- Never put the logo directly on a busy photo. Use a white panel.

## Icons
- Indigo line icons on white tiles, one per capability, on a 24 px grid with a 1.6 px stroke and rounded joins. The icon library still has to be redrawn as SVG [placeholder].
