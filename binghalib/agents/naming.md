# Naming

Binghalib brand guidelines for AI agents · https://sbinghalib.github.io/binghalib/agents/naming.md · Updated 2026-10-19
Use when: Writing the group or any company name, in English or Arabic; URLs and file names.
Rules that always apply: https://sbinghalib.github.io/binghalib/AGENTS.md

## Group
- English: **Binghalib Group of Companies**, then "Binghalib Group" after first use. "Bin Ghalib Group of Companies" also appears. Pick one spelling: TBC.
- Arabic: مجموعة شركات بن غالب
- The lowercase "bin ghalib" appears only in the logo artwork. In text write "Binghalib".
- Tagline: "Tomorrow's Technology Today" (keep the apostrophe, title case).
