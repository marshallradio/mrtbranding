# Voice and writing

Binghalib brand guidelines for AI agents · https://sbinghalib.github.io/binghalib/agents/voice.md · Updated 2026-10-19
Use when: Writing or editing English copy: proposals, tenders, web, social, email.
Rules that always apply: https://sbinghalib.github.io/binghalib/AGENTS.md

## Principles
- **Engineered**: lead with specifications people can check (13,000 A, 160 kA for 1 s, Form 4b, IP54, 4,000 valves a year).
- **Proven**: name real clients, projects and accreditations, only the ones this hub lists (for example EIAC ISO/IEC 17025, LB-CAL-023 for AlphaTech Calibration).
- **Forward**: "Tomorrow's Technology Today". Back each claim with an example (AI-generated BOMs, Industry 4.0, GhalibSat).

## Style
- British English, active voice, short sentences. No exclamation marks in formal copy.
- Don't invent figures, clients or awards. Where the catalogue and websites disagree (for example 13,000 A against 10,000 A), ask first.
