# Logo

Binghalib brand guidelines for AI agents · https://sbinghalib.github.io/binghalib/agents/logo.md · Updated 2026-10-19
Use when: Placing the Binghalib Group logo or emblem in any design; choosing a logo file.
Rules that always apply: https://sbinghalib.github.io/binghalib/AGENTS.md

## Group master logo
- Lockup: `/assets/logos/binghalib-group.svg` (default; also `.png`, 3904 px).
- Without shadow: `/assets/logos/binghalib-group-noshadow.svg`. Use it below 200 px wide, in headers and on nameplates.
- Reversed: `/assets/logos/binghalib-group-reversed.svg`. Use it on dark backgrounds (Indigo 95). This version is derived, so confirm it before print.
- Emblem only: `/assets/logos/binghalib-emblem.svg`. Use it for favicons, avatars, nameplates and small square spaces.

## Construction
- Emblem: a lowercase "b" on the face of an isometric cube, with its bowl drawn as a radiating fan.
- Wordmark: "bin ghalib" in Bauhaus (outlined artwork) in Indigo, over a Red rule, with "GROUP OF COMPANIES" beneath.

## Rules
- Clear space equals the height of the "b" bowl on every side. Minimum 140 px on screen and 30 mm in print for the lockup, 16 px for the emblem (proposed values).
- Never stretch, recolour, rotate, retype, add effects, or place it on busy photos.
- Company logos: see /agents/sub-brands.md.
