Welcome to the Binghalib brand hub

If you're here, you shape how Binghalib shows up: on a type-test certificate, a panel nameplate, a tender submission, a WETEX stand or a two-line email.

Binghalib is one group with many companies. Since 1982 it has grown from a single LV switchgear workshop in Sharjah to 16 independent companies across manufacturing, engineering, trading and services. This hub keeps them recognisably one family: the emblem, the colours, the type and the voice.
