Introduction

Brand architecture

Binghalib is an endorsed house of brands. The group emblem, the "b" in a cube, is the master mark. Companies that carry the family name (Engineering, Trading, Technology, Qatar) use the bilingual Binghalib lockup with a descriptor. Acquired or specialist brands (TAMCO, Rolli, SYSCOM, AlphaTech, VBG Intech, MEM, Time Cargo, Marshall) keep their own marks and are endorsed with "A member of Binghalib Group of Companies".

Branded house: family name

  • Binghalib Engineering Enterprises
  • Binghalib Engineering Qatar
  • Binghalib Technology
  • Binghalib Trading

Endorsed brands: own mark plus group endorsement

  • TAMCO · Rolli International · VBG Intech · Intech
  • SYSCOM · BMS · Marshall · Marshall Intech
  • AlphaTech · MEM · Time Cargo Logistics

Brand components

Logo

A cube, a "b" and a turbine

The emblem is a lowercase b on the face of an isometric cube. Its bowl is drawn as a radiating fan, like a turbine or a busbar fan-out. It reads as solid, engineered, and something built. The wordmark "bin ghalib" is set in Bauhaus (ITC Bauhaus style, outlined in the artwork) in Binghalib Indigo, over a Binghalib Red rule, with "GROUP OF COMPANIES" beneath. Company lockups pair the emblem, in a red keyline square, with the Arabic name بن غالب.

Reversed lockup: dark backgrounds

Wordmark and rules in white, cube unchanged. Follows the inverse logo used on binghalib.com. Confirm with the brand owner before print.

Without shadow: small sizes

Drop the cube's soft shadow below 200 px wide, in headers and on nameplates.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the "b" bowl on every side. Minimum widths: 30 mm in print, 140 px on screen for the lockup, 16 px for the emblem alone. proposed values

They identify Manufacturing, Engineering, Trading and Services, so use them for wayfinding only.

Typography

Logo typeface: Bauhaus logo only

The "bin ghalib" wordmark is drawn in Bauhaus: heavy, monoline, round bowls, a single-storey a and g. The Group Profile embeds the face as Bauhaus-Bold for the "ENTERPRISES" descriptor in the Binghalib Engineering lockup. In the Group logo itself the letters are outlined. Treat Bauhaus as locked to the logo. Never set headlines, body copy or new company names in it, and never retype the wordmark. Always use the supplied logo artwork. ITC Bauhaus is a commercial typeface, so the website doesn't load it as a webfont. confirm cut & licence (ITC Bauhaus Demi/Bold)

BauhausLogo only · supplied as artwork · not a webfont

Brand typeface: Outfit

Headlines, UI and body copy are set in Outfit. It's an open-source geometric sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and a single variable font from Thin to Black, bundled with this hub. Its round bowls and single-storey a echo the Bauhaus wordmark without imitating it, so the logo still stands apart. The Group Profile set its text in Poppins, which remains an approved fallback for existing print templates.

Iconography

The catalogue uses indigo line icons on white tiles, one per capability (panel manufacturing, electrical testing, LV commissioning, retrofit, AMC, engineering studies), with an orange hexagon accent on the active domain. Keep a 24 px grid, a 1.6 px stroke and rounded joins. icon library to be redrawn as SVG
