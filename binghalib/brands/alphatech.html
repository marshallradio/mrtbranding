Company profile

"One of the leading distributors of test & measurement tools in UAE". It is the authorised distributor of Fluke and other brands, and runs an online store you can search by brand, type and characteristics.

The website describes AlphaTech as "a member of Bin Ghalib Group" (research notes).

Logo

Always use the supplied artwork. Don't redraw, retype, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects. In the Group Profile every company logo sits on a white panel, including over photography. Do the same on dark or busy backgrounds.

Usage notes

  • Keep clear space of at least the height of the logo's main capital (or "b" bowl) on every side. proposed
  • Minimum width 30 mm in print and 120 px on screen. proposed
  • Put it on white or very light neutrals. Use a white panel on any other background. No reversed (white) version has been supplied TBC.
  • Use the company palette below only. Don't mix it with another company's signal colour.

Group endorsement

AlphaTech is an endorsed brand. It keeps its own mark and colours. The group link is shown with an endorsement line, "A member of Binghalib Group of Companies", next to or below the company logo.
