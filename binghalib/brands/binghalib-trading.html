Authorised distributor of LV switchgear components (ABB, LS, Rockwell Automation, Hager, Wago) from a Dubai warehouse.

Company profile

Supplies complete low voltage switchgear components. It is an authorised distributor of ABB, LS, Rockwell Automation, Hager and Wago, and focuses on the electrical distribution, panel building and OEM markets.

The catalogue mentions substantial inventory and centralised, automated warehousing in Dubai, delivering within the UAE and worldwide.

Logo

Always use the supplied artwork. Don't redraw, retype, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects. In the Group Profile every company logo sits on a white panel, including over photography. Do the same on dark or busy backgrounds.

Usage notes

  • Keep clear space of at least the height of the logo's main capital (or "b" bowl) on every side. proposed
  • Minimum width 30 mm in print and 120 px on screen. proposed
  • Put it on white or very light neutrals. Use a white panel on any other background. No reversed (white) version has been supplied TBC.
  • Use the group palette only. Don't mix it with another company's signal colour.

Group endorsement

Binghalib Trading is a family-name company: its lockup already carries the Binghalib emblem, the "bin ghalib" wordmark and the Arabic name, so it needs no separate endorsement line. Use the lockup as supplied, in group Indigo and Red.

With other group companies, place it alongside the group lockup at equal height. Never add a second emblem.
