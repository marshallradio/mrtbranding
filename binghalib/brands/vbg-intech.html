Company profile

Designs and manufactures control valves for general and severe service: superheated steam, high differential pressure, volatile and corrosive substances, erosive conditions and slurry. It also offers solutions for cavitation and high noise.

The facility has a capacity of 4,000 control valves a year, over 5,000 m² of temperature-controlled space, CNC machines, and in-house engineering, design, machining, welding, painting and testing. Product range: BG and BR series control valves. Projects include ADNOC (EPCN for WAVE C3B) and PDO (Shammar, Mirrah Solar, Fahud Gas Compression).

Logo

Always use the supplied artwork. Don't redraw, retype, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects. In the Group Profile every company logo sits on a white panel, including over photography. Do the same on dark or busy backgrounds. The catalogue only has a raster version, so ask for the master files before print.

Usage notes

  • Keep clear space of at least the height of the logo's main capital (or "b" bowl) on every side. proposed
  • Minimum width 30 mm in print and 120 px on screen. proposed
  • Put it on white or very light neutrals. Use a white panel on any other background. No reversed (white) version has been supplied TBC.
  • Use the company palette below only. Don't mix it with another company's signal colour.
