BMS · Btech

Building management & ELV systems: BMS, lighting control, GRMS, BTU metering, energy management.

Company profile

Building and energy management. The catalogue describes "energy saving intelligent building control" that brings IT and building automation onto one converged IP-based platform.

Products and solutions include BMS, GRMS, lighting control, structured cabling and metering, with integration of HVAC, lighting, CCTV, fire, access control and energy management. Projects include Abu Dhabi Airport MTB, the ENOC Expo 2020 "station of the future", Nakheel Avani Hotel and Fakeeh University Hospital.

Logo

Always use the supplied artwork. Don't redraw, retype, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects. In the Group Profile every company logo sits on a white panel, including over photography. Do the same on dark or busy backgrounds.

Usage notes

  • Keep clear space of at least the height of the logo's main capital (or "b" bowl) on every side. proposed
  • Minimum width 30 mm in print and 120 px on screen. proposed
  • Put it on white or very light neutrals. Use a white panel on any other background. No reversed (white) version has been supplied TBC.
  • Use the company palette below only. Don't mix it with another company's signal colour.
