TAMCO Windows + Doors

uPVC windows, doors, curtain walls, skylights and conservatories using German profiles (UAE–German JV).

Company profile

Complete solutions for uPVC windows and doors with high-quality German profiles. The catalogue claims energy savings of up to 20%.

Products: uPVC windows, doors, curtain walls, skylights and conservatories, plus wooden roller shutters and glass. The website describes TAMCO as a UAE–German joint venture.

Logo

Always use the supplied artwork. Don't redraw, retype, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects. In the Group Profile every company logo sits on a white panel, including over photography. Do the same on dark or busy backgrounds.

Usage notes

  • Keep clear space of at least the height of the logo's main capital (or "b" bowl) on every side. proposed
  • Minimum width 30 mm in print and 120 px on screen. proposed
  • Put it on white or very light neutrals. Use a white panel on any other background. No reversed (white) version has been supplied TBC.
  • Use the company palette below only. Don't mix it with another company's signal colour.

Group endorsement

TAMCO Windows + Doors is an endorsed brand. It keeps its own mark and colours. The group link is shown with an endorsement line, "A member of Binghalib Group of Companies", next to or below the company logo.
