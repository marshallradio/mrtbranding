Logo

A cube, a "b" and a turbine

The emblem is a lowercase b on the face of an isometric cube. Its bowl is drawn as a radiating fan, like a turbine or a busbar fan-out. It reads as solid, engineered, and something built. The wordmark "bin ghalib" is set in Bauhaus (ITC Bauhaus style, outlined in the artwork) in Binghalib Indigo, over a Binghalib Red rule, with "GROUP OF COMPANIES" beneath.

This page covers the group master logo only. Company lockups (emblem in a red keyline square with the Arabic name) are on each sub-brand page.

Logo versions

Group lockup: use most of the time

Corporate documents, the group website, events, and anywhere several companies appear together.

Emblem only: small or square spaces

Favicons, avatars, panel nameplates, the catalogue's page-corner mark.

derived · confirm

Reversed lockup: dark backgrounds

Wordmark and rules in white, cube unchanged. Follows the inverse logo used on binghalib.com. Confirm with the brand owner before print.

Without shadow: small sizes

Drop the cube's soft shadow below 200 px wide, in headers and on nameplates.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the "b" bowl on every side. Minimum widths: 30 mm in print, 140 px on screen for the lockup, 16 px for the emblem alone. proposed values
